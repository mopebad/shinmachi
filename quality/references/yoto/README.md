# Yoto After Dark research and rights

Inspected 4 November 2026. References inform an original fictional microdistrict; no Tokyo shop names, logos, photographs or map artwork were reproduced as game assets.

| Primary reference | Architectural use |
|---|---|
| [GO TOKYO — Shinjuku Golden Gai](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/62/index.html), [entrance photograph](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/62/images/main.webp) | Small, low-rise bars, narrow thresholds, independent sign identities, exposed service additions and close building edges. The photograph was downloaded temporarily and inspected; the west side retains its small existing rooms, with lower selected roofs, less uniform glazing/awnings and the renamed 月影 bar. |
| [Google Arts & Culture — The Bar-Hopping Culture of Shinjuku Golden Street](https://artsandculture.google.com/story/WAXxlmkjSQH_Jg) | Individual small venues and stair entrances form an intimate cultural environment. This informs a compact listening bar and distinct business identities, rather than a generic neon club row. |
| [GO TOKYO — Shinjuku](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/destinations/western-tokyo/shinjuku/index.html) | Kabukicho's larger entertainment buildings provide the contrasting reference for the east-side multi-tenant facade and stacked directories. Yoto does not copy Kabukicho's road layout. |
| [GO TOKYO — Omoide Yokocho](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/63/index.html), [night alley photograph](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/63/images/main.webp) | Warm entrance pools, readable small signs, overhead services and occupied counter rooms. The photograph was downloaded temporarily and inspected. The project retains its original lantern models and theatre identity. |
| [Omoide Yokocho association — English map](https://www.shinjuku-omoide.com/wordpress/images/pdf/omoyokomap_en.pdf) | Public two-page association map: compact premises related to connected walking passages. Used as spatial context, not copied as a Shinmachi plan. Business hours listed there are not presented as current operating advice. |
| [GO TOKYO — Tokyo at Night](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/story/guide/tokyo-at-night/index.html) and [Jonglez publisher's Soul of Tokyo preview](https://jonglezpublishing.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/Preview-SO-TOKYO_EN.pdf) | Recorded-music bars are distinct from live clubs. The publisher describes Grandfather's as a subdued basement record bar, with wood, records and a carefully selected listening identity. This supports the compact room, visible decks/speakers and low-key atmosphere; its branding and interior layout are not reproduced. The venue's own website timed out during verification. |

The GO TOKYO photographs are copyright Tokyo Convention & Visitors Bureau and are linked for research only. Temporary inspection copies remain outside the repository. The Arts & Culture story and association map are linked, not redistributed as textures or artwork. The project claims no licence to reuse their photographs commercially.

## Recorded music

Both complete original MP3 recordings were obtained from the composer's own catalog, whose track page generates CC BY 4.0 attribution. [The licensing page](https://incompetech.com/music/royalty-free/licenses/) confirms the free attribution-based Creative Commons option. [Local provenance](../../../public/assets/music/provenance.json) records source URLs, ISRC, size and SHA-256 for each unchanged recording.

Electronic recording for 深度 / SHINDO.
