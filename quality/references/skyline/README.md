Additional primary references consulted online:

- [Minato City Tourism Association: 100 views](https://visit-minato-city.tokyo/en/articles/487): different framed, interrupted, distant and reflected landmark relationships. Used as composition guidance, not copied fictional geography.
- [JNTO: Tokyo Tower](https://www.japan.travel/en/spot/1709/): 333 m height, 150 m and 250 m observation decks, Minato/Shiba context and nearby rail access.
- [Official tower illumination](https://www.tokyotower.co.jp/lightup/): warm structural floodlighting and cooler illumination variants. This iteration uses restrained warm winter-inspired emissive steel, without reproducing an event programme.
- [GO TOKYO area guide](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/destinations/southern-tokyo/tokyo-tower-and-around/index.html): surrounding districts, parks and rail access, consulted online. The indexed [Minato City tourist map](https://www.city.minato.tokyo.jp/documents/123403/minatocitytouristmap2026_en2.pdf) was also located, but its PDF returned HTTP 403; no map content is claimed as inspected or redistributed.

Original image-host downloads returned HTTP 429. The public thumbnail host successfully supplied the licensed previews above; no access restriction was bypassed. The procedural models remain original simplified interpretations, rather than a reconstruction of any photographed block.
