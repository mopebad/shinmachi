## Infrastructure and geographic context

- [JR East: seismic reinforcement programme](https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/e/press/20120301/index.html): concrete elevated structures, column reinforcement, and the relationship between reinforcement works and under-track premises. Informed the consistent concrete-pier/steel-portal construction language and pier jackets.
- [JR East 2011 annual report: operations and infrastructure](https://www.jreast.co.jp/e/investor/ar/2011/pdf/ar_2011_08.pdf): railway infrastructure context. Copyright JR East; linked for research, not redistributed or used as a texture.
- [GO TOKYO: Hibiya OKUROJI](https://www.gotokyo.org/en/spot/1846/index.html): the Shimbashi–Yurakucho under-rail commercial context. Its historic brick arches belong to a different structural type; Shinmachi retains concrete piers and straight girders.
- [JR East 2014/15 investor presentation](https://www.jreast.co.jp/e/investor/pdf/2015_02q_presentation.pdf), Tamachi–Shinagawa development map: historical geographic reference for rail corridors beside city blocks and service areas. It is not presented as a current map or an exact plan for Shinmachi. Copyright JR East; linked only.
- [JR East station maps](https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/multi/stations/?lng=en) and [Tamachi station listing](https://timetables.jreast.co.jp/en/timetable/list0976.html): station context and geographic cross-checks. No official map artwork, logos or passenger information assets were copied.

The implemented signage, area guide, building geometry and infrastructure meshes are original procedural assets. Existing Shinmachi materials and sign atlases are reused. No external image request is required at runtime.
